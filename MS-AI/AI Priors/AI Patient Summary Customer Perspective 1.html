AI Patient Summary

Patient history, summarized for faster review

This customer-facing view explains how the AI Patient Summary feature helps users quickly understand a patient's prior exams, spot important findings, and move from a detailed record to a concise decision-ready summary.

Summary at a glance Detailed history view Critical findings highlighted Designed for fast review

What the customer gets

The feature turns multiple prior exams into a single readable history that surfaces the most important findings first. Instead of opening each exam one by one, the user can review a patient-level summary and drill into details only when needed.

Faster reviewImportant findings are grouped and highlighted for quick understanding.
Better contextUsers can see how the current exam relates to the patient’s earlier history.
Clear prioritizationCritical or abnormal items are visually separated from routine information.
Two levels of detailSummary view for quick use, detail view for deeper review.

How it feels in the UI

From the customer’s perspective, the experience is straightforward: open the patient history area, review the summary, and switch to detailed history when a closer look is required.

When the job startsThe AI patient summary job starts after an exam is finalized, so the report is generated automatically in the background.
Where to access itCustomers can open the report from the Previous Exams tab, where the AI History Summary and AI History Details are shown.
Patient History SummaryConcise narrative with major findings and next-step guidance.
Patient History DetailsExam-level data with structured findings and severity markers.
Clinical alertsImportant conditions are called out early so they are hard to miss.
Actionable layoutUseful for radiology, clinical review, and follow-up conversations.

Why it matters

Less scanning, more understanding

The user does not have to jump across multiple reports to reconstruct the patient story.

Better focus on important findings

Critical and abnormal items are surfaced early so they can be prioritized in the review process.

Consistent experience
